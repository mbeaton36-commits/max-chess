Learn chess

A beginner-friendly path through the fundamentals.

The pieces

The king moves one square. The queen moves in any straight line or diagonal. Rooks move straight, bishops move diagonally, knights jump in an L-shape, and pawns advance forward and capture diagonally.

Opening principles

1. Fight for the center. Move a central pawn to claim space.

2. Develop your minor pieces. Bring knights and bishops into the game.

3. Keep your king safe. Castle early when it is sensible.

4. Don't bring your queen out too early. Develop your pieces before launching an attack.

Practice room

Small habits make a stronger player.

Before each move, ask yourself:

  1. Is my king safe?
  2. Can I capture something for free?
  3. What is my opponent threatening?
  4. Will my piece be safe on the new square?

About MaxChess

Chess should be fun and accessible.

MaxChess is a free chess-learning project with a playable board, beginner lessons, board themes, and move history. No subscription is needed.

This is an independent project inspired by the clean, community-focused style of modern chess sites; it is not affiliated with Lichess.
